# Ableton Push Sampling

## Monitor

1. Push `Mix` twice, to access `Input & Output`
2. Choose `Monitor > In` to hear what's playing from the sample source
3. Choose `I/O > Input` and `Type > Ext. In`

The input volume can be boosted in `Preferences > Audio`, for an iPhone, boost each `Line` gain to `+20 dB`

## Record

1. On an `Audio Track`, hold `Record` and push the track button to arm it
2. Push `Record` to start recording

## Convert to Instrument

Push `convert` then choose a destination instrument (e.g., `Simpler`, `Drum Rack`, etc...)
